What is a Gnomon?

The Gnomon is the primary shadow-casting structure in a sundial (seen as the large triangular wall in the Samrat Yantra). Its sloped hypotenuse edge is built exactly parallel to the Earth's axis of rotation, which allows the sun's shadow to accurately measure local solar time regardless of the time of year.

1. Foundation & Orientation

The Geographic Location dictates the instrument's alignment. Use the True North/South orientation details in the Structure block to lay the foundation accurately.

2. Inclination & Slopes

The Mathematical Variables provide exact angles for the masonry. The gnomon wall, for instance, must precisely match the location's latitude angle to track celestial objects correctly.

3. Scale & Dimensions

The Structure Details give you the physical measurements. The base radius determines the overall size and scale of the structure, ensuring mathematical proportions are maintained.
